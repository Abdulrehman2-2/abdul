At the start of the hand (Preflop):

It looks up its 2 cards in a built-in 6-tier strength chart (Tier 1 = best hands like AA, KK, AKs; Tier 6 = weakest).

Using that tier, it decides whether to raise, call, or fold (or if chips are low at 12 big blinds or less, it uses its All-In / Fold chart).

"When the flop hits" (3 shared cards are dealt on the table):

Now the bot starts running up to 1,500 simulations to calculate its exact chance of winning/equity.

It runs those 1,500 simulations again on the turn (4th card) and the river (5th card) to decide whether to raise, call, check, or fold.
